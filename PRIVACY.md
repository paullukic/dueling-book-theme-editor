# Privacy Policy — DuelingBook Theme Editor

Last updated: 2026-10-11

DuelingBook Theme Editor does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

## What is stored

- Your settings (theme, colours, keyboard shortcuts, muted usernames, alert choices, window positions).
- Your notes, action log, background image and custom CSS, if you use those features.

All of this is kept in your browser's extension storage on your device. Settings also sync between your own browsers if you use Chrome sync. You can export, import or erase everything from the Support tab ("Reset all settings"), or by removing the extension.

## What is sent

Nothing about you. The extension only reads from DuelingBook's own image server (images.duelingbook.com): card pictures for test hands, and the playmat artwork it recolours for the full theme. These are the same public files the site itself loads. Theme codes you share are text you copy yourself; the extension never sends them anywhere.

## Permissions

- **storage**: to remember your settings and notes.
- **unlimitedStorage**: so a large background image fits on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
- **scripting**: to re-attach the extension to DuelingBook tabs that were already open when it was installed or updated.
- The extension runs only on duelingbook.com.
